TJC Identity Guidelines Reference

Official Standards from identity.tjc.org

1. Official Theme Colors (TJC Blue / Tekelet)

Pantone 288 C (Primary Blue)
HEX: #23356F
RGB: 35, 53, 111 · CMYK: 100, 91, 27, 15
Pantone 314 C (Secondary Blue)
HEX: #007DA4
RGB: 0, 125, 164 · CMYK: 87, 41, 22, 1

"The color blue (in Hebrew, tekelet) is heavenly. It represents the true church is of heaven and is preaching the complete gospel... Different versions of blue or teal should not be used." (Identity Guidelines p. 25)

2. Prescribed Typographic System

Primary Sans Serif: Source Sans Pro / Source Sans 3 General UI, Web, Buttons

Designed to communicate with confidence and authority while remaining friendly and approachable.

Primary Serif: Crimson Text Formal communications, Scriptures, Articles

Gentle, traditional oldstyle serif with superior legibility for longform reading.

Secondary Serif: Playfair Display Titling & Headlines

High-contrast display serif suited for editorial and heroic headlines.

3. Brand Architecture

"We are one church. We are a unified body. We have one identity. We are a branded house, not a house of brands." (Identity Guidelines p. 21)
