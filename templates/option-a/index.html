The Word of God

The Ten Basic Beliefs

Rooted purely in the inspired Holy Scriptures, confirming the apostolic gospel preached by the disciples in the New Testament.

01. Jesus the One True God 1 Tim 3:16

Jesus Christ, the Word who became flesh, died upon the cross for the redemption of sinners, was resurrected on the third day, and ascended to heaven. He is the only Savior of mankind, the Creator of the heavens and earth, and the only true God.

02. The Holy Bible 2 Tim 3:16

The Holy Bible, consisting of the Old and New Testaments, is inspired by God, the only written truth, and the standard for Christian living.

03. Complete Salvation Eph 2:8

Salvation is given by the grace of God through faith. Believers must rely on the Holy Spirit to pursue holiness, to honor God, and to love humanity.

04. Holy Spirit Baptism Acts 2:4

Receiving the promised Holy Spirit, evidenced by speaking in unknown tongues, is the guarantee of our inheritance in the Kingdom of Heaven.

05. Water Baptism John 3:5

Water baptism is the sacrament for the remission of sins and for regeneration. It must be administered in natural living water, with head bowed and facing downward, in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ.

06. Footwashing John 13:8

The sacrament of footwashing enables one to have a part with the Lord Jesus. It also serves as a constant reminder of love, holiness, humility, forgiveness, and mutual service.

07. Holy Communion 1 Cor 10:17

The Holy Communion is the sacrament to commemorate the death of the Lord Jesus Christ. It enables us to partake of the flesh and blood of our Lord and to be in communion with Him, so that we may have eternal life.

08. The Sabbath Day Gen 2:2 · Ex 20:8

The Sabbath day, the seventh day of the week (Saturday), is a holy day, blessed and sanctified by God. It is to be observed under God's grace for the commemoration of God's creation and salvation, with eternal rest in mind.

09. The Church Eph 1:23

The True Jesus Church, established by our Lord Jesus Christ through the Holy Spirit during the time of the latter rain, is the restored true church of the apostolic times.

10. Final Judgment 2 Thess 1:7

The Lord’s Second Coming will take place on the last day when He descends from heaven to judge the world: the righteous will receive eternal life, while the wicked will be condemned eternally.
