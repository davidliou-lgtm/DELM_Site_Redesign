The 10 Basic Beliefs

Read Full Doctrinal Treatise →
1. Jesus the One True God 1 Tim 3:16

Jesus Christ, the Word who became flesh, died on the cross for the redemption of sinners, was resurrected on the third day, and ascended to heaven. He is the only Savior, Creator of heaven and earth, and the only true God.

2. The Holy Bible 2 Tim 3:16

The Holy Bible, consisting of Old and New Testaments, is inspired by God, the only written truth, and the authoritative standard for Christian life and doctrine.

3. Complete Salvation Eph 2:8

Salvation is given by the grace of God through faith. Believers must rely on the Holy Spirit to pursue holiness, honor God, and love one another.

4. Holy Spirit Baptism Acts 2:4

Receiving the promised Holy Spirit, evidenced by speaking in unknown tongues, is the guarantee of our inheritance in the Kingdom of Heaven.

5. Water Baptism John 3:5

Water baptism is the sacrament for the remission of sins and regeneration. Administered in living water with head bowed, in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ.

6. Footwashing John 13:8

The sacrament of footwashing enables one to have a part with the Lord Jesus, and teaches love, holiness, humility, forgiveness, and mutual service.
