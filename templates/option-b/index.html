Apostolic Foundations

The 10 Basic Beliefs of the True Jesus Church

Every doctrine preached by the True Jesus Church is firmly grounded in the inspired Word of God.

01

Jesus the One True God

The Word who became flesh, died for redemption, resurrected, and ascended. He is the Creator and only Savior.

1 Tim 3:16 · Col 2:9
02

The Holy Bible

The Old and New Testaments, inspired by God, the only written truth, and the authoritative standard for Christian life.

2 Tim 3:16 · 2 Pet 1:21
03

Salvation by Grace

Salvation is given by the grace of God through faith. Believers must rely on the Holy Spirit to pursue holiness and obedience.

Eph 2:8 · Titus 3:5
04

Holy Spirit Baptism

Receiving the promised Holy Spirit is evidenced by speaking in tongues, guaranteeing our heavenly inheritance.

Acts 2:4 · Rom 8:16
05

Water Baptism

Sacrament for remission of sins and regeneration, performed in living water with head bowed, in the Lord Jesus' name.

Acts 2:38 · John 3:5
06

Footwashing

Sacrament enabling fellowship with the Lord, teaching mutual love, holiness, humility, service, and forgiveness.

John 13:1-17
07

Holy Communion

Sacrament commemorating the death of the Lord Jesus with one unleavened bread and grape juice, having eternal life.

1 Cor 10:16 · John 6:53
08

The Sabbath Day

The 7th day of the week (Saturday) is a holy day blessed and sanctified by God, kept in grace to commemorate creation and salvation.

Exod 20:8 · Luke 4:16
09 & 10

The Church & Second Coming

The true church restored in the latter rain; Christ will descend on the Last Day to judge the world and give eternal life.

Joel 2:23 · 1 Thess 4:16
