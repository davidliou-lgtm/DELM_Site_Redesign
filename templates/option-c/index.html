Weekly Rhythm of Worship

Sabbath Worship Schedules

Following the apostolic practice, True Jesus Church observes the 7th-day Sabbath from sunset Friday to sunset Saturday.

Friday Evening 7:30 PM – 9:00 PM

Sabbath Eve Fellowship & Prayer

Welcoming the holy Sabbath day with hymn singing, scripture meditation, topical bible study, and earnest kneeling prayer in the Spirit.

Format: Hymns (15m), Sermon (50m), Prayer (20m)
Attendees: All ages, youth fellowship sessions
Primary Service
Saturday Morning 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Sabbath Morning Divine Service

The focal point of weekly worship. Includes pastoral sermon, multilingual audio translation, and communal prayer of intercession.

Fellowship: Complimentary communal lunch at 12:00 PM
Broadcast: Global livestream available
Saturday Afternoon 1:30 PM – 3:30 PM

Afternoon Worship & Sabbath School

Afternoon sermon followed by age-graded Sabbath School classes for toddlers, youth, and young adults.

Classes: Kindergarten, Elementary, J/S High, Seeker Class
Choir: Rehearsals conclude after service
Visiting for the First Time?

What to Expect at a TJC Service

We know visiting a new church can feel unfamiliar. Here is a clear, welcoming guide to our worship traditions.

01

Attire & Arrival

We dress with modest and respectful attire honoring God’s presence. Greeters at the foyer will hand you a bulletin and hymnal.

02

Kneeling Prayer

Before and after service, we kneel on prayer cushions. We pray in the Spirit (speaking in unknown tongues) or say "Hallelujah" in quiet meditation.

03

Expository Sermons

Our messages center on verse-by-verse scripture exposition from the Bible. Feel free to open your Bible or use our provided pew Bibles.

04

Fellowship Lunch

After Saturday morning service, all guests and members share a complimentary lunch. It is a wonderful chance to ask questions and make friends.

Doctrinal Summary

The 10 Basic Beliefs

Full Doctrinal Articles →
01. One God
Jesus the True God
The Word became flesh, died for redemption, resurrected, and is the only Savior.
02. Scripture
The Holy Bible
Old and New Testaments inspired by God, the only written standard for faith.
03. Grace
Salvation
Given by grace through faith; believers must rely on the Spirit to pursue holiness.
04. Spirit
Holy Spirit Baptism
Evidenced by speaking in unknown tongues, guarantee of heavenly inheritance.
05. Sacrament
Water Baptism
Remission of sins in living water, head bowed, in Lord Jesus' name.
06. Sacrament
Footwashing
Enables fellowship with the Lord Jesus, teaches mutual love and humility.
07. Sacrament
Holy Communion
Commemorates the Lord's death with one unleavened bread and cup of juice.
08. Holy Day
The Sabbath Day
Seventh day of the week (Saturday), blessed and sanctified by God in creation.
09. Body
The True Church
Restored by God through the Holy Spirit in the time of the latter rain.
10. Hope
The Second Coming
Christ will descend to judge the world; righteous receive eternal life.
