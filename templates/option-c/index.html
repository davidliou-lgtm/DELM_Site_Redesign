Welcoming Guide

What to Expect When You Visit

Visiting a church for the first time should be peaceful and uplifting. Here is an overview of what our services look like.

01

Warm Welcome

Our ushers will greet you at the entrance, provide a Bible and hymnal, and help you find comfortable seating in the sanctuary.

02

Kneeling Prayer

We kneel before God in humility when we pray, following the apostolic practice. You are warmly welcome to kneel or sit quietly.

03

Scripture Preaching

Sermons are grounded directly in the Holy Scriptures, centered on Jesus Christ, righteousness, repentance, and eternal salvation.

04

Fellowship Meal

Following the Saturday morning service, all visitors and brethren share a complimentary home-cooked lunch fellowship together.

Biblical Truth

10 Basic Beliefs at a Glance

The fundamental apostolic doctrines taught by the True Jesus Church.

1
Jesus the One True God

Jesus is the Creator of heaven and earth, who died for our sins and was resurrected.

2
The Holy Bible

The inspired word of God, complete in the Old and New Testaments, the sole standard of truth.

3
Complete Salvation

Given by grace through faith in Christ, requiring holiness and reliance on the Holy Spirit.

4
Holy Spirit Baptism

Evidenced by speaking in unknown tongues, the guarantee of our heavenly inheritance.

5
Water Baptism

For the remission of sins, administered by full immersion in living water with head bowed.

6
Footwashing

Sacrament to have part with the Lord, instilling mutual love, humility, and forgiveness.
